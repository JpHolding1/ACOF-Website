Health & Housing Program

Providing whole-person care approach services to support tenant health and wellbeing

What We Provide

The Health and Housing Program provides ACOF tenants opportunities to participate in services relating to the whole-person care approach. Tenants receive healthy lifestyle and coaching opportunities, including nutrition education, meditation, yoga, respiratory health, ensuring connection to healthcare service(s), and so much more.

Services provided in the Health and Housing program are completely voluntary. The goal of the program is to provide a wide variety of health services that tenants are interested in and find meaningful.

Program Benefits

Community Building

The health opportunities allow tenants to gather with one another and not only learn from the person hosting the class but also from their neighbors. They discuss what works for them and what doesn't.

Preventing Isolation

The program fosters a sense of community that helps prevent isolation which can negatively impact mental health.

Daily Application

Tenants can bring the practices they learn into their daily life whether it is daily cooking, mindfulness practices at home, or decluttering their home to have a positive impact on their overall health and wellbeing.

Intensive Case Management Services (ICMS)

What is it?

ACOF has recently contracted with DHS (Department of Health Services) to provide you with the Intensive Case Management Services Program. This program will provide you with easy access to health services, whether it is for your physical health, mental health, and/or substance use recovery/treatment. This program is completely FREE, voluntary, and confidential.

Who is eligible?

Any tenant who resides in an ACOF building that is assigned to receive ICMS services. This program will benefit those with complex health conditions or who need a high level of collaborative treatment.

Program Includes

  • Develop a plan for your health needs
  • Help access community resources
  • Coordinate services to improve your personal growth and overall health
  • Ensure you are managing your medication effectively
  • Combined behavioral health and medical care services
  • Monitor your ongoing behavioral, physical, and support needs
