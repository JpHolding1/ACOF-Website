Children Program

Empowering families and youths through opportunities for leadership and cooperative learning

Objective/Mission

The Family and Children Service Program fosters learning opportunities that nurture cognitive, social, emotional, and physical development to empower families and youths through opportunities for leadership and cooperative learning. We tailor our approach to address families' needs and provide a safe space for all to flourish and explore.

Learning Objectives

  • Participants will have an increase in self-awareness and self-management.
  • Participants will recognize and design community involvement activities.

Program Features

Broaden Horizons

We offer uniquely innovative opportunities for learning while celebrating the exploration of interests and differences through enrichment activities, including the arts, music, and fashion, all of which serve to restore hope and foster a greater sense of resiliency in our children.

Holistic Approach

Tailored to promote cooperative learning, balanced nutrition, and the encouragement of active living, student-centered activities enhance confidence while helping in the development of forming positive relationships with other youths and with adults.

Hope for the Future

We enter every year with an understanding that equipping our children with vital tools of success can only be achieved with continuity of services, support from case managers, volunteers, interns, and agency partners who aid to facilitate change beyond obstacles.
