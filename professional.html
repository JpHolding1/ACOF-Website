Professional Development Program

Helping tenants develop skills for employment and personal growth

Program Overview

Image: Tenants at a training workshop

The program is designed to give tenants a better understanding of self-improvement goals and opportunities in the 21st century. The program will encourage and challenge tenants to think creatively and will help tenants harness their unique skills and mindset to adapt better within themselves and the community.

The program emphasizes increasing self-efficacy, confidence, resume writing, communication, and mock interviews.

Overall Program Goals

To provide professional development services to tenants who wish to enhance and enrich their lives with meaningful opportunities whether that be through employment, seeking volunteer opportunities, returning to school to fulfill individual educational goals, or other professional development opportunities within specific boundaries of each individual unique situation.

12-Week Workshop Series

Image: Group learning or resume writing

Weeks 1-3

  • Introduction of Course
  • Social Security Benefits
  • TTW Program
  • Cost of Living in California

Weeks 4-8

  • Employment Search
  • Resume Building
  • Cover Letter Writing
  • Workplace Skills
  • Conflict Management

Weeks 9-12

  • Soft Skills Development
  • Volunteer Opportunities
  • Educational Goals
  • Opportunity Searching

Weeks 13-15

  • Mock Interview Preparation
  • Mock Interview Panels

Week 16

  • Program Culmination
  • Certificate Presentation
  • Post-Assessment
